About our clinic

Because every pet deserves the best care.

Best Vet Clinic and Pet Shop is based in Kuleshwor, Kathmandu. Our purpose is to make compassionate veterinary care and everyday pet essentials easier for local pet families to access.

Our motive

We aim to protect animal health, support early prevention and timely treatment, and help pets live happier, healthier lives. We also want to guide pet owners with helpful consultations and make responsible pet care more accessible through our clinic and pet shop.
